# Instagram vs. TikTok: The Shocking Truth

You've been told you need to be on social media. But which platform actually drives appointments?

After analyzing 50+ medical practices over 6 months, we have a clear answer.

## TikTok Wins (And It's Not Even Close)

Here's why:

### 1. The Algorithm Favors New Accounts

On Instagram, you need 10,000 followers to get any real reach. On TikTok, your first video can hit 100,000+ views if it's good.

We had a client (chiropractor in Denver) post ONE video explaining "why your lower back hurts when you sit." It got 847,000 views and led to 63 booked appointments in 2 weeks.

### 2. Search Intent is Higher on TikTok

People use TikTok like Google. They search "how to get rid of acne" or "is back surgery worth it"—and they want answers from real doctors.

Instagram is more about lifestyle and inspiration. TikTok is about education.

### 3. Your Face = Trust

Video builds trust 10x faster than photos. When patients see you explaining their exact problem in a 45-second video, they feel like they know you before they even call.

## The Instagram Trap

Most doctors post on Instagram because it feels "professional." But here's the reality:

- Your engagement rate is probably under 2%
- Reels get buried unless you already have a huge following
- The "aesthetic" content doesn't drive appointments

## What to Do Instead

**If you only have time for one platform, choose TikTok.**

Post 3x per week. Answer patient questions. Be direct. Use trending sounds.

You don't need fancy editing. You just need to show up consistently and provide value.

One of our clients got 14 new patients last month from TikTok. Her Instagram? Zero.

The data doesn't lie.
