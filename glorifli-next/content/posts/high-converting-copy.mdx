# Why "Holistic Health" Is Killing Your Conversions

You are an expert. You know that "holistic health" means treating the whole person. But to a patient with a migraine that won't go away, "holistic health" is just noise.

## Speak to the Pain, Not the Solution

Patients don't wake up at 3 AM searching for "integrative medicine." They search for "how to stop chronic migraines" or "relief for back pain."

### The "Mirror" Technique
Your website copy should act as a mirror. When a patient reads your headline, they should feel like you are describing their exact situation.

**Bad:** "Comprehensive Wellness Solutions for All Ages."
**Good:** "Finally Break Free From Chronic Back Pain Without Surgery."

### Specificity Builds Trust
When you are specific about the symptoms and the struggle, the patient automatically assumes you have the solution. 

## Keywords for AEO
Specific copy isn't just good for humans; it's essential for AI. Answer Engines like ChatGPT look for specific answers to specific questions. By writing detailed, problem-focused content, you position your practice as the authority.
