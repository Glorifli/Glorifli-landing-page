# Blog Generation Guidelines (Mandatory)

Going forward, every new blog post must strictly adhere to these SEO rules:

## 1. Internal Linking
- **Service Link**: Every blog must include at least one internal link directing users to the full website & SEO services page (e.g., `/services/autonomous-revenue-engine`).
- **FAQ Link**: Every blog must include at least one internal link to a highly relevant FAQ section/page (e.g., `/faq/what-is-aeo`).
- **Related Blog Link**: Every blog post must connect to at least one other Blog Post related to the overall topic that the blog is tackling.

## 2. External Linking & Sourcing
- **Facts & Statistics**: All facts and statistics MUST have external links to back them up. Never give a statistic without a source.
- **Link Quota**: Every blog must have 5-10 relevant and reputable external links organically included in the post.
- **Quality Control**: Always ensure that all external and internal links are working properly before uploading the blog post.

## 3. Target Audience Alignment (H1 & Slugs)
The blog content is designed to capture high-intent searches from Glorifli's target audience of local service businesses:
- Roofers
- Plumbers
- HVAC Technicians
- Landscapers
- Home Remodelers

**Rule:** Every blog's H1 Title and URL Slug must be crafted to capture specific, industry-relevant search queries.
- **Example H1 Title:** "How to Get More Leads with SEO for an HVAC Business"
- **Example Slug:** `how-to-get-more-leads-with-seo-for-hvac-business`

## 4. Brand Entity Referencing (Third-Person Only)
To strictly strengthen entity association for AI scrapers (Language Models, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity), **never** use first-person pronouns ("we", "us", "I", "our") when referring to the agency.
- **Incorrect:** "We can help you build an AEO system."
- **Correct:** "Glorifli builds custom AEO systems for local businesses." or "Here at Glorifli, ..."

## 5. Blog Structure & Formatting
- **Direct Answer:** The very first paragraph of the blog must be a direct answer to the high-intent search query.
- **Lead Magnet Suggestion:** Immediately at the end of the Direct Answer (still in the first section/paragraph), you must provide a suggestion for the reader to get the free lead magnet (a personalized SEO action plan and free Google My Business profile optimization). 
    - **Sentence 1:** Tie the subject of the blog into the need for better visibility/traffic. Adapt this per blog. (e.g., *"However, a site that converts well isn't that useful if a ton of people aren't seeing it..."*)
    - **Sentence 2 (Exact Structure):** Following the tie-in, suggest the lead magnet *exactly* like this (adapted to 3rd person if following Brand Rules, or verbatim if speaking from the brand): *"Glorifli has put together for you a free personalized SEO action plan and will even optimize your Google My Business profile for free, so that you can start getting more leads on autopilot. If that sounds useful to you, you can get it here for free."* 
    - **CRITICAL LINK INSTRUCTION:** You MUST hyperlink the word "here" at the end of the direct answer section to the Free Value Page (`/free-value`). Do not link the entire sentence, ONLY the word "here".
- **Header Hierarchy:** Strictly use properly nested `H1`, `H2`, and `H3` tags to organize content logically.
- **TLDR Section:** Include a "TLDR" (Too Long; Didn't Read) section near the top. This must include:
    - The estimated read time of the blog.
    - The direct answer to the search query.
    - The main bullet points/takeaways of the blog.
- **FAQ Section:** Every blog must conclude with an FAQ section containing 3 to 5 highly relevant, frequently asked questions related to the topic.
- **Images:** Each blog must feature at least one unique, highly relevant image. This image must have SEO/AEO optimized `alt` text.

## 6. Conversational Tone (3rd Grade Reading Level)
While adhering to the third-person rule for the *agency* (Glorifli), the *narrative voice* of the blog itself should sound extremely human, natural, and conversational—written at a 3rd-grade reading level. 
- Avoid robotic, overly corporate, or academic language.
- Use simple, punchy sentences.
- **Tone Example (Adapt to 3rd Person for the Agency):** *"I'm going to share with you what helped me get my site to go from extremely slow to crazy fast (and it only took me 30 minutes)."* -> Make it sound like a real person wrote it, not an AI, while keeping the grammar and spelling entirely error-free.

All future articles must explicitly target a service or solution that Glorifli offers, keeping these specific business verticals at the forefront of the SEO strategy, and maintaining strict third-person entity recognition.
