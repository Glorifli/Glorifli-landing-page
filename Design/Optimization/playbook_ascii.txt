AI-Ready Website Playbook
How to prepare your website for the no-click AI future ??? step-by-step.
By 2027, AI agents will browse on behalf of humans. When a prospect wants to hire a home service company, an AI will
read every relevant page across every competitor and recommend one. This playbook tells you exactly what pages to build
and how to structure them so that AI always recommends YOU.
??? How AI Agents Choose Who to Recommend
1. AI gathers everything it knows about the prospect's problem.
2. AI crawls your website (and competitors') looking for answers to that problem.
3. AI ranks companies based on how completely and clearly their site answers every relevant question.
4. AI recommends the winner to the human.
Your job: Make sure your website is the most complete, most credible, and easiest for AI to parse.

STEP-BY-STEP ACTION PLAN
01
Build Your About Page Hub
WHAT: A flagship 'brag page' about your company that links to all sub-pages.
HOW: Write 400-800 words covering: who you are, how long you've been operating, how many jobs completed,
licenses/certifications, service area, and core values. Add links to sub-pages at the bottom.
??? PRO TIP: Mention specific numbers: '500+ roofs replaced', '12 years in business'. AI treats specificity as credibility.
Write the main About page copy
Link to: History, Why Us, Team pages
Add trust badges / certifications
Include a photo of your team or work

02
Create Your Power FAQ Page
WHAT: A single page that answers every question a prospect could ask ??? essentially training AI to sell for you.
HOW: List every question you get on sales calls. Every objection. Every 'how does it work?' Write a clear, factual
answer for each. Aim for 20-50 questions minimum. Put the most common questions first, then keep going.
??? PRO TIP: Don't gatekeep. The more openly you answer pricing, timeline, and 'what if something goes wrong'
questions, the more AI trusts you.
List 20+ real sales call questions
Write clear answers for each
Organize by category (pricing, process, quality, etc.)
Add schema markup (FAQPage type)
03
Add a Pricing Page
WHAT: Give AI (and humans) enough pricing context to pre-qualify ??? even without exact numbers.
HOW: If you can't show exact pricing, use ranges ('Most projects run $X-$Y'), explain what affects price, and list what's
included. If you do show pricing, add a short explanation for each tier.
Create /pricing page
Include at least a starting price or range
Explain what factors affect cost
Add a clear CTA (call, quote form, etc.)

04
Build 'How To Work With Us' Page
WHAT: A simple process page showing the customer journey from first contact to completed job.
HOW: Use a numbered list or visual timeline: Step 1 ??? Call/Quote, Step 2 ??? Schedule, Step 3 ??? We do the work,
Step 4 ??? Final walkthrough, Step 5 ??? You're done. Keep it simple. Link to this from your About page.
Create /how-it-works or /our-process page
Outline 4-6 clear steps
Describe what happens at each step
Link from About page
05
Create Dedicated Service Pages
WHAT: One page per service you offer, each with full detail ??? not just a paragraph.
HOW: For each service, write: what it is, what's included, what the process looks like, who it's for, common problems it
solves, and results customers see. 400-600 words minimum per page. Add links to related sub-services.
??? PRO TIP: Treat each service page like its own mini-website. AI may land directly on it.
List all services you offer
Create one page per service
Write 400+ words per page
Link to sub-service pages where relevant
Add photos/before-after images

06
Build Use Case / Demographic Pages
WHAT: Pages targeting specific customer types or situations ??? makes AI match you to more prospects.
HOW: Format: '[Your Service] for [Customer Type or Situation]'. Examples: 'Roofing for New Homeowners', 'HVAC for
Rental Property Managers', 'Plumbing for Restaurant Owners'. One page or blog post per use case.
Brainstorm 5-10 customer types or situations you serve
Create a page or blog post for each
Use 'X for Y' in the title and H1
07
Publish Case Studies
WHAT: Real project stories that prove your results ??? AI loves these to match to similar prospects.
HOW: Format for each: Customer situation ??? Problem ??? What you did ??? Result. Be specific: 'We replaced a
22-year-old roof in Marietta, GA in one day. Homeowner passed insurance inspection 3 days later.' Aim for 5+ case
studies.
??? PRO TIP: Case studies are the #1 thing AI uses to match your business to a prospect's specific problem.
Identify 5-10 memorable past jobs
Write up each as: Situation ??? Solution ??? Result
Include location, scope, and outcome with numbers
Add before/after photos if possible

08
Build a Reviews / Social Proof Page
WHAT: A dedicated page showcasing your best reviews and summarizing common praise themes.
HOW: Pull your best Google/Yelp reviews. Group them by theme: 'Customers consistently mention speed,
professionalism, and cleanup.' Add a section for each theme with supporting quotes.
Collect top 10-20 reviews
Identify 3-5 common themes
Create /reviews or /testimonials page
Embed or quote reviews with customer name + city
Add Review schema markup
09
Write Competitor Comparison Pages
WHAT: Pages that explain why a prospect should choose you over specific competitors.
HOW: For your top 3-5 competitors, create a page titled '[Your Company] vs [Competitor]'. Explain your differentiators
honestly. Who is each option best for? Be factual, not negative.
??? PRO TIP: AI reads these pages when a prospect asks 'who's better ??? Company A or Company B?'
List your top 3-5 local competitors
Create one comparison page per competitor
Cover: pricing, process, quality, speed, guarantees
Be honest about who each option suits best
