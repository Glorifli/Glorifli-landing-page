# How do I rank my business on Google Maps?

To rank in the "Local Pack" (Top 3), you must optimize your **Google Business Profile (GBP)** for completeness and activity. key factors are **Primary Category selection**, **Review Velocity** (new reviews weekly), and **Entity Consistency** (Name, Address, Phone match everywhere).

Your website is secondary. Your Maps profile is primary.

## Why am I invisible on Google?

If you aren't in the top 3, you are invisible.

*   **Incomplete Profile:** You missed the "Services" section.
*   **Wrong Category:** You chose "Consultant" instead of "Business Management Consultant".
*   **Zero Activity:** You haven't posted a photo in 6 months.

## The Google Maps Ranking Checklist

1.  **Claim & Verify:** Do this today.
2.  **Hours of Operation:** Must be 100% accurate.
3.  **Photos:** Upload 5 photos of your team/office. No stock photos.
4.  **The "Q&A" Section:** Seed this with 5 common questions and answer them yourself.

## How do I get more reviews?

You have to ask.

*   **Automate it:** Send a text 1 hour after the service.
*   **Be specific:** "Please mention [Service Name] in your review." (This helps SEO).

## What about my website?

Your website backs up your Maps profile.

Ensure your **NAP (Name, Address, Phone)** is in the footer of every page.
