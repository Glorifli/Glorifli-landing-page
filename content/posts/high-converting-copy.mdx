# How do I write copy that sells?

To write sales copy that actually converts, stop talking about yourself ("We are established in 2010...") and start talking about the client's problem. Use the **PAS Framework**: Identify the **Problem**, **Agitate** the pain of that problem, and then offer your service as the **Solution**.

Your client is the hero. You are the guide.

## The PAS Framework Explained

### 1. Problem
Call out the issue directly.
*   *Bad Copy:* "We offer SEO services."
*   *Good Copy:* "Are you invisible on Google Maps?"

### 2. Agitate
Twist the knife. Make them feel the cost of inaction.
*   *Bad Copy:* "Rankings are important."
*   *Good Copy:* "Every day you aren't on Page 1, your competitors are stealing your best leads."

### 3. Solution
Present your offer as the only logical fix.
*   *Bad Copy:* "Hire us."
*   *Good Copy:* "Our Local SEO system puts you in the top 3 in 90 days."

## Be Specific.

*   **Vague:** "We save you time."
*   **Specific:** "We save you 15 hours a week."

Specifics sell. Generalities fail.
