# Why Generic Copy Is Killing Your Conversions

You are an expert. You know that "holistic" means solving the whole problem. But to a client with a specific issue that won't go away, "holistic" is just noise.

## Speak to the Pain, Not the Solution

Clients don't wake up at 3 AM searching for "integrative solutions." They search for "how to fix [specific problem]" or "help with [challenge]."

### The "Mirror" Technique
Your website copy should act as a mirror. When a client reads your headline, they should feel like you are describing their exact situation.

**Bad:** "Comprehensive Business Solutions for All Industries."
**Good:** "Finally Fix Your Staffing Issues Without Overpaying."

### Specificity Builds Trust
When you are specific about the challenges and the struggle, the client automatically assumes you have the solution. 

## Keywords for AEO
Specific copy isn't just good for humans; it's essential for AI. Answer Engines like ChatGPT look for specific answers to specific questions. By writing detailed, problem-focused content, you position your business as the authority.
