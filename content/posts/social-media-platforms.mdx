# Which social media platform is best for business?

**TikTok** is currently the best platform for new client acquisition because its algorithm reaches strangers (non-followers). **Instagram** is better for nurturing existing relationships. If your goal is growth, prioritize TikTok and repurpose to Instagram Reels.

The era of the "curated feed" is dead. The era of the "algorithmic feed" is here.

## Why does TikTok convert better?

It comes down to **Search Intent**.

People use TikTok like Google. They search "How to fix [Problem]". If you have a video answering that, you win.

On Instagram, people scroll for lifestyle. On TikTok, they scroll for dopamine and information.

## The Viral Math

*   **Instagram Reach:** You reach 10% of your followers.
*   **TikTok Reach:** You reach 100,000 strangers who don't follow you yet.

## What is the strategy?

Do not create for two platforms. Create for one.

1.  Film in vertical 9:16 format.
2.  Post to TikTok first (use trending audio).
3.  Download (remove watermark) and post to Instagram Reels.
4.  Post to YouTube Shorts.

## Trust the Data

One of our clients posted the same video to both.
*   Instagram: 400 views.
*   TikTok: 847,000 views.
