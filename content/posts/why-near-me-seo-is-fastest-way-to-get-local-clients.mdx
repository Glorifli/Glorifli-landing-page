Getting into the Local Pack and dominating "Near Me" searches requires a specific AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) infrastructure—so that both Google and AI assistants trust you as the best local answer.

Your Name, Address, and Phone number must be identical across every platform on the internet. Inconsistencies confuse search engines, weaken trust, and can even trigger ranking issues in local results.

Recent local SEO guides note that Google uses a huge mix of on‑page, link, and citation signals to rank local businesses, with NAP consistency and completely verified profiles standing as the core architectural prerequisites. You need a steady stream of fresh, 5‑star reviews that mention specific services (e.g., "Best roof repair in the area!").
