## How Does ChatGPT Decide Which HVAC Company to Recommend?

ChatGPT and other AI assistants recommend businesses based on three primary signals: **entity authority**, **review consensus**, and **content clarity**. Here's what each means for your HVAC business:

**Entity Authority** is how well-defined your business is across the internet. If your company name, address, phone number, and services are consistently listed on 50+ platforms — Google Business Profile, Yelp, HomeStars, Angi, Facebook, your own website — AI systems recognize you as a verified, trustworthy entity. According to **[Moz's Local Search Ranking Factors](https://moz.com/local-search-ranking-factors)**, citation consistency is one of the top signals for local entity trust.

**Review Consensus** is the aggregate signal across Google, Yelp, Facebook, and HomeStars. When an AI tool checks "who's the best HVAC company in Calgary" and finds that one business has 4.9 stars across 200+ reviews while competitors have 3.8 stars and 12 reviews, the recommendation writes itself.

**Content Clarity** means your website explicitly answers the questions AI systems are being asked. A page titled "Emergency HVAC Repair in [City]" with clear pricing information, service descriptions, and structured FAQ content is far more likely to be cited than a homepage that just says "We fix your HVAC."

## Step 1 — Build a Complete, Verified Google Business Profile

Your Google Business Profile (GBP) is the primary data source that feeds AI search recommendations for local queries. Without a fully optimized GBP, you're invisible.

According to **[Google's own guidance on Business Profiles](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091)**, complete profiles are dramatically more likely to be considered relevant for local searches. For AI-powered answers, the GBP is the first dataset the algorithms verify against.

What "fully optimized" means for your HVAC company:

*   All service categories checked — "HVAC Contractor," "Air Conditioning Contractor," "Heating Contractor," "Furnace Repair Service"
*   Every service listed with descriptions and price ranges
*   10+ high-quality photos of your team, trucks, and completed jobs
*   Business hours accurate, including emergency availability
*   The Q&A section pre-seeded with answers to the 5 most common HVAC questions
*   A "Book an Appointment" button linked to your booking system
*   Weekly posts — even one photo of a completed job per week signals active engagement

## Step 2 — Get Your Business Listed on 50+ Directories

AI tools don't just read your website. They cross-reference your business identity across dozens of platforms to decide how confident they are in recommending you.

**[BrightLocal's Citation Research](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-citations-trust-report/)** shows that businesses with citations on 50+ relevant directories rank significantly higher in local search and appear more frequently in AI-generated local recommendations. The key is consistency — every listing must have your business name, address, and phone number in the exact same format.

The top directories for HVAC companies:

*   Google Business Profile (non-negotiable)
*   Yelp, Facebook, Angi, HomeAdvisor
*   HomeStars (Canada) or Houzz
*   BBB (Better Business Bureau)
*   Bing Places for Business
*   Apple Maps
*   YellowPages, Foursquare, Nextdoor
*   Industry-specific: ACCA (Air Conditioning Contractors of America)

## Step 3 — Add Schema Markup to Your Website

Schema markup is the code that tells AI systems and search engines exactly what your business is and what it does in machine-readable format. Without it, AI tools have to guess — and they often get it wrong or skip you entirely.

The schema types your HVAC website needs:

*   **LocalBusiness** with subtype "HVACBusiness" — explicitly tells Google and AI you are an HVAC contractor
*   **Service** — one schema per service (AC repair, furnace installation, duct cleaning, etc.)
*   **FAQPage** — makes your FAQ content eligible for AI citation and Google's People Also Ask
*   **Review/AggregateRating** — surfaces your star rating in search results

According to **[Schema.org's official guidance](https://schema.org/HVACBusiness)** and case studies from **[Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/schema-markup-local-seo/)**, businesses using complete LocalBusiness schema see 20–30% improvements in local search visibility.

## Step 4 — Publish AEO-Optimized Content Monthly

The reason AI systems cite certain HVAC companies over others in content queries is simple: those companies have published clear, direct answers to the questions homeowners are asking. An AI tool looks for content that directly answers the query. If your site has a blog post titled "How much does HVAC replacement cost in [City]?" with a clear, factual answer in the first paragraph — you're exactly what AI systems are looking for.

**[HubSpot's content marketing research](https://www.hubspot.com/marketing-statistics)** consistently shows that businesses publishing 8+ quality blog posts monthly generate 3.5x more traffic than those publishing 4 or fewer. For AEO specifically, the format matters as much as the frequency:

*   Start every post with a direct 40-word answer to the question
*   Use question-based H2 and H3 headings throughout
*   Include at least one cited statistic per section
*   Add FAQPage schema to every post
*   Link internally to your main service pages

## Step 5 — Generate Reviews Consistently

Reviews are both a local ranking factor and an AI trust signal. When ChatGPT weighs two HVAC companies — one with 14 reviews and one with 180 — the recommendation is clear. **[BrightLocal's 2025 Consumer Review Survey](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey/)** shows that 87% of consumers rely on online reviews as much as personal recommendations for local services.

The fastest way to build reviews:

*   Text every customer a direct Google review link within 30 minutes of job completion
*   Verbally ask at the end of every job: "If you're happy with the work, it would mean a lot if you left us a quick Google review"
*   Respond to every review within 24 hours — positive and negative
