## The Problem with Buying Shared Roofing Leads

For years, roofers have relied on lead-generation platforms like HomeAdvisor, Angi, or Thumbtack. Here is why that aggregate model is actively hurting your business:

1.  **They Are Not Exclusive:** When a homeowner submits a request for a roof inspection, that exact lead is sold to you and 3 to 5 of your direct competitors simultaneously. 
2.  **It's a Race to the Bottom:** Because the homeowner is bombarded by five different contractors within three minutes, the conversation automatically devolves into a price war. 
3.  **You Don't Own the Asset:** You are renting your pipeline from a third party. If you stop paying them tomorrow, your business starves.
