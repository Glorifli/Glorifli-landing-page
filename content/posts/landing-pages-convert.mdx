# What makes a landing page convert?

A high-converting landing page must have **One Clear Goal**, **Above-the-Fold Value Proposition**, **Social Proof**, and a **Zero-Friction Call to Action (CTA)**. The average landing page converts at 2.35%. Top performers convert at **11.45%** by removing distractions and focusing on the user's problem.

Pretty design does not sell. Clarity sells.

## The 5-Point Landing Page Checklist

1.  **The Headline:** Does it state the benefit in 3 seconds? ("Get More Leads" vs "Digital Solutions").
2.  **The Sub-headline:** How do you do it? ("We build automated systems...")
3.  **The Hero Image:** Does it show a happy result?
4.  **Social Proof:** Do you have testimonials visible without scrolling?
5.  **The CTA:** Is the button a contrasting color? ("Book Now").

## Why is my bounce rate high?

You are likely confusing the visitor.
*   Too many links in the navigation? Remove them.
*   Slow load speed? Fix images.
*   Generic copy? Be specific.

## The "Grunt Test"

Show your page to a stranger for 5 seconds. Turn off the screen.
Can they tell you **what you do** and **how to buy**?
If not, rewrite it.
