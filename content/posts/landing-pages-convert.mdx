# Landing Pages That Convert: A Business Owner's Guide

Your website is your 24/7 salesperson. But if it's not built to convert, you're losing thousands in potential revenue every month.

## The #1 Mistake Businesses Make

Most business websites are digital brochures. They list services, show credentials, maybe have some stock photos. But they don't *ask for the appointment*.

**A high-converting landing page does one thing**: it removes every barrier between a visitor and booking.

## 5 Essential Elements

1. **Clear Headline**: State the problem you solve, not your credentials
2. **Social Proof**: Real client testimonials (with photos if possible)
3. **One Clear CTA**: "Book Now" should appear at least 3 times
4. **Trust Signals**: Certifications, years of experience, media features
5. **Easy Booking**: Embedded calendar or 1-click phone call

## Real Example

A consultant we worked with went from 12 monthly bookings to 47 by changing her headline from "Experienced Consultant in Austin" to "Solve Your [Specific Problem] in 6 Weeks (or Your Money Back)".

Specific. Outcome-focused. Risk-reversed.

That's how you convert.
