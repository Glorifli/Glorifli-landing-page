# How do I get more clients in 2026?

To get more clients in 2026, you must optimize for **Answer Engines (AI)**, not just Search Engines. The top 5 strategies are: **AEO Structuring** (answering questions directly), **Local SEO Domination** (Maps), **Short-Form Video Trust**, **Zero-Friction Booking**, and **Automated Nurture Sequences**.

Keyword stuffing is over. "Helpful Content" is the only metric that matters.

## 1. Master "Answer Engine Optimization" (AEO)

AI like ChatGPT wants answers, not links.

*   **Strategy:** Start every blog post with a direct definition (like this one).
*   **Why:** AI snippets will cite you as the source.

[See an example of AEO content](/blog/business-growth-stalled).

## 2. Dominate Local SEO

If you are local, you live and die by Google Maps.

*   **Strategy:** Post photos weekly to your Google Business Profile.
*   **Why:** Activity signals relevance to the algorithm.

[Get the full Local SEO checklist](/blog/seo-for-businesses).

## 3. Build Parasocial Trust

Video is non-negotiable.

*   **Strategy:** Post 3x/week answering common questions.
*   **Why:** Clients trust faces, not logos.

[Learn the video playbook](/blog/short-form-video).

## 4. Zero-Friction Booking

Stop using "Contact Us" forms.

*   **Strategy:** Implement direct calendar booking.
*   **Why:** 70% of clients book outside business hours.

## 5. Automate the Nurture

Leads go cold in 5 minutes.

*   **Strategy:** AI Chatbots that reply instantly.
*   **Why:** Speed to lead increases conversion by 391%.

[Install an AI chatbot today](/blog/chatbots-increase-bookings).
