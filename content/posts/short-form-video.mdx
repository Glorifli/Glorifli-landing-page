# Does short-form video work for business?

Yes. Short-form video is the most efficient way to build **Trust** at scale. Data shows that landing pages with video convert **80% higher** than those without. It allows potential clients to verify your expertise and "meet" you before they book.

It is not about dancing. It is about education.

## Why do I need to be on camera?

People buy from people.

Text is dry. Text can be faked. Video is raw.

When a client watches 3 of your videos, they feel like they know you. This is called a **Parasocial Relationship**. It lowers the barrier to entry.

## What content should I post?

Stop overthinking. Use the **Q&A Method**:

1.  **The Hook:** Read a common client question ("How much does X cost?").
2.  **The Answer:** Give the direct answer in 10 seconds.
3.  **The Explanation:** Spend 40 seconds maximizing value.
4.  **The Call:** Tell them to book a consult.

## Which platform is best?

*   **TikTok:** Best for reach and new audiences. [Read the comparison](/blog/social-media-platforms).
*   **Reels:** Best for nurturing existing followers.
*   **YouTube Shorts:** Best for long-term SEO.

## How do I repurpose content?

One video = 4 assets.

1.  Post to TikTok.
2.  Post to Reels.
3.  Post to Shorts.
4.  Transcribe it into a blog post.
