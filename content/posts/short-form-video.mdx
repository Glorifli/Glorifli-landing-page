# Short-Form Video: The Secret Weapon for Medical Practices

If you think TikTok is just for teenagers, you're missing out on the biggest opportunity in medical marketing.

## Trust at Scale

Video is the only medium that allows a stranger to feel like they know you in 60 seconds. For a doctor, trust is the currency of conversion.

### What to Post?
You don't need to dance. You just need to educate.
1.  **React to News:** "Did you see this study on Vitamin D? Here's my take."
2.  **Debunk Myths:** "Stop doing this if you have knee pain!"
3.  **Behind the Scenes:** Show your friendly staff and clean office.

## Validating Your Expertise
When a potential patient visits your website and sees a library of helpful videos, their anxiety drops. They see you as a human, an expert, and a guide.

## Repurposing for SEO
Take that 60-second script and turn it into a blog post (just like this one!). Now you have content for social media *and* Google.
