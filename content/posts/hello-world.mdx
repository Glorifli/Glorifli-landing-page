# How Do I Get More Patients for My Medical Practice?

Every doctor asks this question. You know you can help people heal, but those people can't find you. The answer isn't just "be a better doctor"—it's about building a strategic online presence that puts you in front of the patients who need you most.

## The Three Pillars of Patient Acquisition

After working with hundreds of doctors, we've identified three non-negotiable pillars:

1. **Visibility:** Patients can't book if they can't find you.
2. **Trust:** Your website must immediately communicate expertise and care.
3. **Conversion:** Once a patient lands on your site, booking an appointment should be effortless.

## Pillar 1: Get Found on Google

[Local SEO is the foundation](/blog/seo-for-doctors) of patient acquisition. When someone searches "doctor near me," you need to be in the top 3 results. This means optimizing your Google My Business profile, collecting reviews, and creating content that answers patient questions.

## Pillar 2: Build Trust Through Content

Patients don't trust generic marketing. They trust doctors who educate them. [Video content](/blog/short-form-video) and [social media](/blog/social-media-platforms) help you build authentic connections before the patient ever walks through your door.

## Pillar 3: Convert Visitors into Patients

A beautiful website means nothing if it doesn't convert. [Your landing page](/blog/landing-pages-convert) needs clear calls-to-action, social proof, and [AI-powered tools like chatbots](/blog/chatbots-increase-bookings) to capture leads 24/7.

## The Complete Strategy

The most successful medical practices don't rely on one strategy—they combine SEO, content marketing, and conversion optimization into a cohesive system.
