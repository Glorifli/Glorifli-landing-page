### Frequently Asked Questions

**Why is mobile optimization important for local SEO?**
Google uses **mobile-first indexing**, meaning it looks at your mobile site *first* to determine your ranking. If your mobile site is slow, fragile, or breaks under pressure, Google will aggressively unrank you, regardless of your desktop site aesthetics.

**What is AEO and how does it help local business?**
AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) ensures your business is the direct answer when someone asks Siri or Alexa, "Who is the best plumber near me?" It focuses on providing direct, factual answers that AI can read. Learn more at [What is AEO?](/blog/what-is-aeo).

**How do I get more Google Reviews?**
You need to automate the ask. After every appointment, your system should automatically send a text requesting a review. This builds the "Review Velocity" needed to rank in the Local Pack. Check out our [Reputation Management Services](/services/reputation).

**Does online booking replace my receptionist?**
No, it empowers them. It handles the routine bookings so your staff can focus on high-value customer service and complex queries. It's about efficiency, not replacement. Read our [Automating Business Operations](/blog/automating-business-operations).

**What is the "Local Pack"?**
The Local Pack is the block of three business listings with maps that appears at the top of Google search results. Ranking here is critical because it captures the majority of local clicks. Learn how to rank there in our [Local Map Ranking Guide](/blog/local-map-ranking-guide).
