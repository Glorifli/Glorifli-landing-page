# SEO for Doctors: Why You're Invisible on Google

When someone searches "dermatologist near me" or "family doctor Austin TX", are YOU showing up?

If the answer is no, you're handing thousands of potential patients to your competitors every single month.

## The Truth About Medical SEO

Medical SEO isn't about stuffing keywords into your website. It's about **proving to Google that you're the most relevant, trustworthy result** for a specific search.

Here's what actually matters:

## 1. Google My Business (This is 80% of the Game)

Your GMB profile is more important than your website for local search. If it's not optimized, you're done.

**Must-haves:**
- Accurate business hours
- High-quality photos (exterior, interior, team)
- 20+ recent reviews with 4.5+ stars
- Weekly posts (yes, really)

## 2. On-Page SEO Basics

Your website needs:
- Title tags with location + specialty: "Austin Dermatologist | Acne & Anti-Aging Specialist"
- H1 headers that match search intent
- Service pages for EACH treatment you offer
- Schema markup (this tells Google you're a medical practice)

## 3. Patient Reviews = SEO Gold

Google rewards businesses with fresh, consistent reviews. Automate your review request process. We recommend asking every patient 2 days after their appointment.

## The 90-Day SEO Plan

- Week 1-2: Claim and optimize GMB
- Week 3-4: Audit your website for technical SEO
- Week 5-12: Publish 1 blog post per week targeting patient questions

If you do this consistently, you'll see results within 90 days. That's not a guess—that's what we've seen with 100+ medical practices.
